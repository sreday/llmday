LLMday Ambassadors

Ambassadors are key community members who help us make LLMday amazing
and bring it to cities around the world. Nominate LLMday Ambassador!
Mansi More
Data Analyst
Qualitest
United States - West

Attending LLMday San Francisco was such a fun and energizing experience. I spend a lot of my time building with LLMs and explaining them to others, but being in a room full of people doing the same thing at different levels just hit differently. The conversations felt real, the demos were practical, and I walked away with ideas I actually want to try, not just bookmark. What I enjoyed most was how community-driven everything felt. As someone who loves teaching, speaking, and making AI more approachable, it reminded me why I started doing this in the first place. I’d genuinely love to contribute more, whether that’s speaking, running workshops, or just helping more people get comfortable building with AI.

LLMday feels like exactly the kind of space where those conversations can turn into meaningful partnerships.

Alecia Cotterell
Head of Careers
Teesside University London
United Kingdom

As someone who supports students in preparing for the professional world, I’m always on the lookout for spaces to connect with industry in a meaningful way. When I first met the LLMday Team, I was struck by their keenness to support the next generation of tech enthusiasts. Students have opportunities to gain practical insight from their theoretical knowledge, can speak directly with practitioners, and develop better understanding of the realities of modern tech careers. Also, it’s been refreshing to be part of something that is encapsulating the true intersection of technology in life with the exploration of untraditional topics within tech such as neurodiversity, mental health, and the importance of mentorship and the early careers sector for the future of industry. Needless to say – I was intrigued! Since then, we’ve set up several internship programmes and I’m looking forward to being part of LLMday ambassadorship to help shape brighter futures for our students. Lastly, and importantly, they are a genuine bunch of lovely people – which is imperative when it comes to community building!
