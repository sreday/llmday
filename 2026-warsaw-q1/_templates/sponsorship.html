{# ── FAQ button + expandable panel ──────────────────── #}

1. When do I need to confirm my sponsorship?

We recommend locking it in at least one month before the event. Last-minute is possible, but it can be a bit rushed for your team.

2. Do sponsors get free conference passes?

Yes. The number of passes depends on your setup. Some teams send one speaker, others bring a bigger team. We'll figure it out together.

3. Can I invite my own network or community?

Yes. We can offer a few free passes for key people, plus discounted tickets for your wider community.

4. You say you run "community events" - what's that at the core?

Most attendees are hands-on people working on real problems every day. It works best if you bring team members who understand their world and can have real conversations, providing solutions for their struggles.

5. How many people should we send?

It's up to you. However, for an event of around 100 people, we'd suggest no more than five people from your team.

6. When can we set up our booth?

You can arrive on the day, up to one hour before the event starts. Booth/table setup usually takes 15 to 30 minutes.

7. Can we bring a bigger screen for our booth?

We provide a 24-inch monitor with HDMI and USB-C connectors. If you want a bigger TV, you can rent it out yourself, just check with us first so we make sure it fits your booth space.

8. Do you offer discounts for multiple sponsorships?

We price items separately so you can mix and match, based on the competition and what feels fair. If your budget is tight, we can talk.
