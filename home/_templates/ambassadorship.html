Who's an Ambassador?

An Ambassador is someone who supports our community by spending a bit of their free time helping us grow our events locally. They're volunteers, not employees, people who genuinely care about sharing knowledge, connecting others, and building something positive around the community.

Many of them help shape the direction of the conferences by sharing ideas, feedback, and local perspective. Sometimes it's about improving the attendee experience, sometimes it's simply about making people feel welcome and included.

We truly appreciate everyone who helps us create spaces where people can learn, meet others, and enjoy the experience together.

Why do we need Ambassadors?

There's only so much we can do as a small team. All of our conferences around the world started through invitations from friends in local communities: Matt & Joyce in the US, Birol in Germany, William & Bas in the Netherlands, Rod in Brazil, Poone & Kini in France, Daniel & Xenda in Portugal, Balaji, Siva & Clarence in India… just to name a few!

Sometimes it's about helping with a key strategic decision. Sometimes it's just grabbing a coffee and brainstorming what we should do next. We want to work closely with people and create opportunities together.

That's the spirit behind our Ambassador program, and the kind of "let's go!" attitude we want to support and reward.

LinkedIn tag If allowed by employer — tag yourself as official conference ambassador on LinkedIn
Onboarding buddy As needed — help welcome and guide new ambassadors

What really matters for us

  • This is very much an informal agreement, no ambassador contract will be required.
  • You will be able to provide feedback, and help us make the ambassador program better.
  • We want this to feel positive and fun, not deadline chasing or stress inducing.
  • You're always welcome to opt out of one or two responsibilities if something would be difficult to maintain on your end.
  • The Ambassador program is built on trust and responsibility, so we won't be looking over your shoulder to check every task 🙂
  • At the end of the day, this is about building a cool, supportive community together.

We hope to work with you and are genuinely glad you're here!
