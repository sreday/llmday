## Abstract

LLMs keep improving, but scaling alone runs into real limits when you need grounded physical understanding, long-horizon planning, and reliable action, especially outside clean, text-only environments. This talk explores what's next: world-model approaches that learn from video and interaction, multimodal perception (vision plus time-series/sensors), and agentic & multi-agent systems that connect models to tools and devices. Together, these building blocks point to Physical AI, AI that can perceive, reason, and act beyond chat in the real world.

## Bio

Przemek Pospieszny is an AI Director at EPAM with 18+ years designing and implementing innovative AI solutions for global organizations. He specializes in agentic and generative AI, physical intelligence, and complex/compound AI architectures— bridging advanced R&D with production delivery. He has led and delivered dozens of AI/ML and data science initiatives for leading enterprises across Europe, the UAE, and the US. Previously, he worked in Silicon Valley with Google ATAP on human–machine interaction and sensor-driven ambient intelligence.
