## Abstract

This session will offer insights into practical examples of deploying SLMs on edge devices from mobile phones to cars. We will take a look at frameworks, models and real applications in compute-constrained environments.

He is particularly passionate about bridging the gap between technology and business strategy, translating complex concepts into actionable insights, and driving enterprise modernization through AI.
