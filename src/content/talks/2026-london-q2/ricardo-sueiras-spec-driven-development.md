## Abstract

Spec driven development (SDD) emphasises defining clear specifications before writing any code, helping address some of the limitations we have seen with AI coding tools. After this talk, you will understand the key elements of SDD and how it might be the missing piece in your workflow.

## Bio

I am a developer advocate for open source, currently working at AWS. I am passionate about, and help customers be successful in using open source.
