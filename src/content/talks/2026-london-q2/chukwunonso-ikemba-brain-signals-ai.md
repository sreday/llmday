## Abstract

Your brain produces consistent neural patterns for every action, 'raise hand' fires the same core pathways regardless of why. If AI can decode languages, it can decode brain signals as language. I'll show why 'raise hand' may just be the foundation for the next level in human technological advance.

## Bio

I'm an AI researcher working across healthcare, human–computer interaction, and next-gen neural interfaces. My work includes collaborating with NHS personnel on how AI can improve health literacy in the UK, focusing on real-world impact rather than hype.

I'm also developing a long-term exploratory project on AI-mediated communication between biological signals and digital systems, investigating how neural activity patterns might one day translate into meaningful computer instructions.

My speaking experience is growing, but my sessions are known for being clear, practical, and grounded. No buzzwords, just solid insight into what AI can actually do today and what it might unlock tomorrow.
